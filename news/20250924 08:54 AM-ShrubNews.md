Mining difficulty hit a record high, bolstering security but pressuring inefficient miners.

Technically, BTC trades above its 200-day SMA ($97,352), with RSI at 67.40 signaling mild overbought conditions. Resistance at $117,500 looms, but a breakout could target $122,000–$130,000. Michael Saylor's push for Saudi Arabia's $930 billion fund to invest in BTC could spark surges if realized.
