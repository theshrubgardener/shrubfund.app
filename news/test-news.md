# Welcome to Shrub Fund News Hub

We're excited to launch our new News Hub feature! This automated system will keep you updated with the latest developments, announcements, and insights from the Shrub Fund team.

## What's New

- **Automated News Updates**: Articles are automatically published when uploaded to our GitHub repository
- **Organized by Date**: All articles are sorted by newest first
- **Rich Metadata Support**: Each article can include custom titles, dates, and descriptions
- **Seamless Integration**: Works perfectly with our existing website design

## How It Works

Our news system uses GitHub as a content management system. Simply upload new `.md` files to the `/news/` folder in our repository, and they'll appear here automatically.
